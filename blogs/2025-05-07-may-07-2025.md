Change We just released three new voice features for Chirp 3: HD Voices. Pace control is available across all locales; pause control is available across all locales; custom pronunciations is available across all locales except bn-in, gu-in, nl-be, sw-ke, th-th, uk-ua, ur-in, and vi-vn. Be sure to check our Chirp 3: HD Voices documentation for more information.
